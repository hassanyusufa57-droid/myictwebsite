About the center

An ICT café is a public place where people can use computers and the internet when they do not have their own. Hassan T/Wada ICT Center was set up to make technology easy to reach for students, job seekers, traders and small business owners.

We help people apply for schools and jobs, register for exams, print documents and learn basic computer skills. Our staff are always on hand, so nobody has to struggle alone with a form or a file.

Why people choose us

We keep our prices fair, our machines clean and our internet steady. Backup power keeps your work safe when the light goes off.

  • Reliable internetStable connection for browsing, downloads and online exams.
  • Trained staffHelp with forms, scanning, typing and document layout.
  • Standby powerGenerator and inverter, so work continues during outages.
  • Private and safeYour files and passwords are never kept or shared.
